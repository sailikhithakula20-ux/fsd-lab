# ICMR-NIN Recommended Dietary Allowances (RDA) for Indians, 2020 Edition
# Source: Indian Council of Medical Research - National Institute of Nutrition (ICMR-NIN)
# Reference: "Nutrient Requirements for Indians: A Report of the Expert Group" (2020)

## Energy Requirements (Kilocalories per Day)

### Sedentary Activity Level
- Adult Male (19-30 years): 2110 kcal/day
- Adult Male (31-60 years): 2010 kcal/day
- Adult Female (19-30 years): 1660 kcal/day
- Adult Female (31-60 years): 1600 kcal/day

### Moderate Activity Level
- Adult Male (19-30 years): 2710 kcal/day
- Adult Male (31-60 years): 2580 kcal/day
- Adult Female (19-30 years): 2130 kcal/day
- Adult Female (31-60 years): 2050 kcal/day

### Heavy Activity Level
- Adult Male (19-30 years): 3470 kcal/day
- Adult Male (31-60 years): 3310 kcal/day
- Adult Female (19-30 years): 2720 kcal/day
- Adult Female (31-60 years): 2620 kcal/day

## Macronutrient Distribution (Percentage of Total Energy)
- Protein: 10-15% of total daily energy
- Fats: 20-30% of total daily energy
- Carbohydrates: 55-70% of total daily energy (predominantly complex carbohydrates)
- Dietary Fibre: 40 g/day for adult males, 30 g/day for adult females

## Protein Requirements
- Adult Male (sedentary): 54 g/day
- Adult Male (moderate work): 54 g/day
- Adult Male (heavy work): 54 g/day
- Adult Female (sedentary): 46 g/day
- Adult Female (moderate work): 46 g/day
- Adult Female (heavy work): 46 g/day
- Pregnant Women (second half): additional 7.7 g/day
- Lactating Women: additional 13.2 g/day
- Reference protein requirement: 0.83 g per kg body weight per day (digestibility-adjusted)
- Indian cereal-pulse diet adjustment: approximately 1.0 g per kg body weight per day due to lower digestibility

## Fat Requirements
- Total fat intake: visible fat should not exceed 20-30 g/day for sedentary adults
- Essential fatty acids (EFA): linoleic acid at 4.5% of energy, alpha-linolenic acid at 1.0% of energy
- Saturated fatty acids (SFA): should not exceed 8% of total energy
- Trans fatty acids: should not exceed 1% of total energy
- n-6:n-3 ratio: should be between 5:1 and 10:1
- Cholesterol: limit to below 300 mg/day

## Micronutrient Recommendations

### Calcium
- Adults (19-65 years): 1000 mg/day
- Elderly (>65 years): 1200 mg/day
- Pregnant and Lactating women: 1200 mg/day
- Good Indian sources: ragi (finger millet, 344 mg/100g), sesame seeds (1450 mg/100g), green leafy vegetables, dairy products, amaranth seeds

### Iron
- Adult Male: 19 mg/day
- Adult Female (menstruating): 29 mg/day
- Pregnant Women: 27 mg/day
- Good Indian sources: bajra, ragi, garden cress seeds (halim), jaggery, dark green leafy vegetables (amaranth, drumstick leaves), Bengal gram (chana)

### Vitamin A
- Adult Male: 1000 mcg retinol equivalents per day
- Adult Female: 840 mcg retinol equivalents per day
- Indian sources: carrots, pumpkin, mango, papaya, drumstick leaves, sweet potato, cod liver oil

### Vitamin D
- Adults: 600 IU/day (15 mcg/day)
- Elderly (>65 years): 800 IU/day (20 mcg/day)
- Prevalence of deficiency in India is 50-90% in various populations
- Indian strategy: adequate sunlight exposure (10-15 minutes between 10 AM and 3 PM) plus dietary fortification

### Vitamin B12
- Adults: 2.2 mcg/day
- Pregnant Women: 2.6 mcg/day
- Critical concern for strict vegetarian Indians who avoid all animal products
- Indian vegetarian sources: fermented foods (idli batter, dosa batter contain trace B12 from microbial fermentation), fortified milk, curd

### Folate (Vitamin B9)
- Adults: 300 mcg DFE/day
- Pregnant women: 500 mcg DFE/day
- Indian sources: green leafy vegetables (spinach, fenugreek leaves, amaranth), Bengal gram, rajma, liver

### Zinc
- Adult Male: 17 mg/day
- Adult Female: 13.2 mg/day
- Indian sources: pumpkin seeds, sesame seeds, cashew nuts, chickpeas, lentils

## Hydration Guidelines
- General adult recommendation: 2.0 to 2.5 litres of water per day
- Active individuals and warm climate (Indian conditions): 2.5 to 3.5 litres per day
- Clinical formula: approximately 35 ml per kg body weight per day
- Signs of adequate hydration: pale yellow urine colour, regular urination frequency

## Special Dietary Guidance for Indian Population
- Cereal-pulse combination (e.g., rice + dal, roti + rajma) significantly improves protein quality through amino acid complementation
- Indian vegetarian diets should emphasize adequate protein from pulses, legumes, dairy, and nuts
- Millets (ragi, jowar, bajra, foxtail millet) provide superior glycaemic control compared to polished rice and refined wheat
- Fermented foods (idli, dosa, curd, kanji) improve nutrient bioavailability and gut health
- Traditional Indian spices (turmeric, fenugreek, cinnamon) have evidence-based metabolic and anti-inflammatory properties
- Coconut oil and groundnut oil should be used in moderation and rotated with mustard oil and rice bran oil for balanced fatty acid profiles
