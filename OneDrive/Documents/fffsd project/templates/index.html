CLINICAL MOTIVATION

The South Asian Cardiometabolic Challenge

Epidemiological studies confirm that South Asian populations face an elevated risk of Type-2 Diabetes and premature cardiovascular illness at lower BMI thresholds due to higher visceral adiposity and carbohydrate-heavy diets.

Blunting Postprandial Glucose Spikes

Replacing refined polished rice with finger millet (Ragi) or sorghum (Jowar) reduces the Glycemic Index of meals from >70 to <55, mitigating pancreatic beta-cell exhaustion.

Sodium & Potassium Homeostasis

Traditional Indian pickles and spice mixes often exceed 4,000 mg of sodium daily. Our tracking highlights sodium-potassium balance using foods like tender coconut water and fresh curds.

The Three Pillars of Prevention

How continuous lifestyle data intercepts metabolic decline:

1. Nutritional Fiber & Protein Ratio Target > 30g Fiber

Delays carbohydrate absorption and sustains intestinal satiety hormones.

2. Post-Meal Ambulation 15-min Walking

Stimulates non-insulin GLUT-4 muscular glucose disposal.

3. Circadian Sleep Architecture 7.5–8 Hours

Regulates nocturnal cortisol and preserves morning insulin sensitivity.
