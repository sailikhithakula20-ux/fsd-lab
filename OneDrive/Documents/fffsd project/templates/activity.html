Scientific MET Formulations

One Metabolic Equivalent of Task (MET) represents the resting oxygen uptake ($\approx 3.5\text{ ml O}_2/\text{kg/min}$). Total active energy expenditure is calculated as:

$$\text{Burn (kcal)} = t_{\text{min}} \times \left(\text{MET} \times 3.5 \times \frac{M_{\text{kg}}}{200}\right)$$
